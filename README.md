### On film — Reborn from Shards

[![Reborn from Shards — the documentary site: a documentary film by Ziyang Xu, shot in Jingdezhen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jerryxugit-2026/jerryxugit-2026/main/assets/film-site-hero.jpg)](https://jerryxugit-2026.github.io/Film-Reborn-from-Shards/)

**[▶ Enter the documentary site — Reborn from Shards](https://jerryxugit-2026.github.io/Film-Reborn-from-Shards/)** ·
**[▶ Watch the full film (11:14)](https://youtu.be/Ucu1jr4pBH8)** ·
**[▶ All four cuts](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLT7qnD3BIifg&si=j4C-_Ec37VkbrBwI)**

These films were shot in Jingdezhen and follow the question I first met there:
what happens after porcelain breaks. Fired ceramic
does not decay — a broken
bowl lasts centuries — and the city produces hundreds of thousands of tons of it
a year, buried under old kilns and turned up again by roadworks. I filmed the
people unburying it: Yi Design, who press recycled shards into permeable bricks
and furniture and have stayed unprofitable on purpose ("always do the right
thing, no matter how difficult it is"), and Xun Studio, who test how much
once-fired powder a clay body will tolerate before it stops being plastic. Then
I ran the experiment myself — crushed discarded shards, sieved the powder twice,
mixed two grain sizes at three ratios, and fired 120 test specimens. Only 20
came out intact, and the usable ceiling sat around 10% powder. The films end
where my own work begins. Machines are designed for perfection; I am still
interested in the crooked rim of a hand-made pot.

**The playlist holds four cuts, and each one does a different job:**

- **[2-Minute Highlights (2:44)](https://youtu.be/9qq94EWFfLU)** — the whole arc
  in under three minutes: the problem, the people, the experiment, and the
  question. Start here.
- **[How to make reclaimed ceramics (4:16)](https://youtu.be/cG6fcK39ahE)** — the
  studio experiment on its own: crushing shards, sieving the powder twice, mixing
  two grain sizes at three ratios, and firing 120 test specimens to find that 20
  survive.
- **[Reborn from shards — Full version (11:14)](https://youtu.be/Ucu1jr4pBH8)** —
  the complete documentary: the city and its buried waste, Yi Design and Xun
  Studio, and what happens after porcelain breaks.
- **[Reborn from shards— Editor Choice Version](https://youtu.be/wH9gUHj6f7Y)** —
  a second cut of the same story, released as a YouTube premiere.

### Shards Reborn- Flowing Apple

"Shards Reborn" is an art initiative where I transform broken ceramic
heirlooms into personal memorials. Interviewing family, friends, and teachers.
Apple, my grandmother's favorite fruit. One corner of the apple is cut open,
showing the pink feathers inside. It resembles my childhood memories at her
home, the gentle, tender part of love that remains. I don't want the apple to
be complete or symmetrical, because what I wanted to express was never a
perfect fruit, but a flow of longing that couldn't fit into a neat, regular
form.
